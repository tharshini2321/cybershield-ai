A safer digital habit

Pause. Verify. Then act.

Scam messages often try to rush people into decisions. Take a moment to check who contacted you and whether the request makes sense.

  • Never share an OTP, password, or PIN.
  • Do not trust a link just because it looks familiar.
  • Verify unexpected requests using a known official contact.
  • If money is at risk, contact your bank immediately through its official channel.
